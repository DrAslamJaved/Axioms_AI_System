# Social Media Agent

## Purpose

The Social Media Agent prepares platform-native **drafts** for academic and educational
communication. It supports LinkedIn professional posts, Instagram carousels, X threads,
TikTok short-video outlines, and WhatsApp community-broadcast drafts.

## Inputs and outputs

- Inputs: topic, audience, chosen platforms, objective, approved source scope, author-verified
  facts, voice, optional call to action, calendar length, and recent same-topic post records.
- Outputs: a platform-native draft for every selected platform, asset and accessibility notes,
  a proposed review calendar, publication checks, and a DOCX export.

The proposed calendar is a planning aid only. It does not create a social-media schedule or
communicate with a third-party service.

## Governance contract

- The agent accepts only author-supplied verified facts; it does not discover, infer, or verify
  factual, academic, credential, performance, or outcome claims.
- An exact same-topic post recorded for a selected platform less than 48 hours ago is rejected.
  The author remains responsible for recording recent posts accurately.
- Every visual, clip, audio item, quotation, and dataset needs a confirmed copyright permission,
  licence, or fair-use basis before publication.
- Alt text, captions, text alternatives, readable contrast, and mobile readability are explicit
  publication checks.
- WhatsApp drafts require the author to confirm recipient consent before sending.
- The agent never connects to accounts, schedules posts, publishes content, sends direct or mass
  messages, scrapes platforms, or performs engagement automation.
- Explicit human approval is mandatory before any upload, sharing, scheduling, or publication.
